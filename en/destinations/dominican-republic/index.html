Direct flights

In February 2023 the Dominican Republic and Canada signed an air transport agreement that lets airlines of both countries operate direct flights between any of their airports.

02

Canadian visitors

About 715,000 Canadians visited the country in 2022, according to the Canadian ambassador quoted in Marie-Josée’s article on the air agreement.

03
