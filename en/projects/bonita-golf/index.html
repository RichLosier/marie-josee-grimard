Bonita Golf

Bonita Golf Residences sits directly on the Las Iguanas course in the heart of Cap Cana. Designed by architecture firm GVA, the project has only 160 residences, about 400 metres from Juanillo Beach.

The project page says construction is already well under way. Delivery dates are to be confirmed with Marie-Josée.
