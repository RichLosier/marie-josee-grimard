The project

Palm View

Palm View is a residential complex with hotel services in the Coral Golf Resort community, next to the clubhouse and the 1st and 10th holes of the golf course. One-, two- and three-bedroom condos, furnished, with full management by Hospitality Management Solutions (HMS).

The project is part of the Coral Golf Resort master plan: golf, private beaches and modern infrastructure.

Location

  • 4 min from Bávaro Adventure Park
  • 5 min from shopping and entertainment
