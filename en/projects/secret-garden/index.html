The project

Secret Garden

In Bávaro, a few steps from the beach, Secret Garden is a closed project combining a commercial area, an area of family homes, duplexes and villas, and an area of one-, two- and three-bedroom condos.

The project sits on a 50,000 m² plot: an 1,800 m² pool with a bar, mini-golf, a beach volleyball court, a restaurant, shared workspaces and large garden areas. The Gesproin group is planned as vacation-rental manager.
