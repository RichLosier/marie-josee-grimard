At a glance

Sun Garden

Sun Garden is in the Cabeza de Toro area, inside the new Coral Golf Resort, on the former Catalonia Bávaro Beach Golf & Casino course, restored by P.B. Dye. The project has 192 condos with one, two and three bedrooms, all facing the natural-water lagoon or the golf course.

Residents have access to Pearl Beach Club and the Bávaro Adventure Park activities, and play golf at a preferred rate.
