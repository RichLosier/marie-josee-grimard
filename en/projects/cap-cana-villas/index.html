The project

Cap Cana Villas

A collection of contemporary villas in Cap Cana, surrounded by Nicklaus golf courses, white-sand beaches and a European-style marina. Wood, natural stone and floor-to-ceiling glazing: each villa offers generous indoor and outdoor space.

Each villa includes a private pool, jacuzzi, staff quarters and a BBQ area, on lots from 2,000 m² to more than 3,400 m². The project page mentions about 4.5 hours by air from Montréal or Toronto.

Location

  • Three Nicklaus signature courses
  • Juanillo and Api beaches
  • Cap Cana Marina
  • The Stables and Punta Cana Polo Club
  • Punta Cana airport close by
