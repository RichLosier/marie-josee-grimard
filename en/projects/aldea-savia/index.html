The project

Aldea Savia

Aldea Savia is on Tulum’s Quinta Avenida, the preferred area for wellness tourism. A private community emerging in the Mayan jungle, in four phases over 10 hectares, designed around sustainability principles.

Housing prototypes for every lifestyle: condos, duplexes with rooftop or garden, and houses, surrounded by nature, terraces and rooftops that merge with the green sea of the jungle.

Location

  • Maya Train station
  • Tulum International Airport
  • Tulum downtown and shopping malls
  • Cenotes and Caribbean beaches
