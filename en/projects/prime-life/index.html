The project

Prime Life

Prime Life has 30 luxury two- and three-bedroom apartments across five blocks in Carvoeiro, one of the Algarve’s most charming coastal towns. Each apartment is designed for natural light and open living, with spacious terraces and modern finishes.

Carvoeiro keeps a village scale, with historic streets, marinas, golf courses and sites such as the Benagil Cave.

Location

  • 750 m to Carvoeiro beach
  • 200 m to restaurants and cafés
  • 350 m to public transport
  • 45 min to Faro airport
