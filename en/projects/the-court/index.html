The project

The Court

The Court has 104 apartments in two contemporary buildings, from studios to two bedrooms. Upper-floor units have a private rooftop terrace with jacuzzi, some ground-floor units an exclusive-use garden, with views of the Monchique mountains and the sea.

Amenities: indoor and outdoor pools, a wellness centre with gym, sauna and massage, four padel courts, a multi-sports field, restaurant, bar, concierge and a free shuttle to the beach.

Location

  • Carvoeiro’s beaches, cliffs and cultural attractions
  • 45 min from Faro International Airport
  • Estômbar train station 6.6 km away
